An Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) is the flying aircraft itself, 
while an Unmanned Aircraft System (UAS) includes the aircraft, ground controls, and software, 
and "drone" is the casual term for either

Understanding the Differences

UAV (Unmanned Aerial Vehicle): The physical flying machine with no pilot on board.

UAS (Unmanned Aircraft System): The entire ecosystem, including the UAV, ground control station, software, and data links.

Drone: A general, everyday word used for any unmanned vehicle or flying craft.
